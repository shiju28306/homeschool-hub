Telling Time to the Hour

Ages about 5–7

Read and draw times to the hour. Learn that the long minute hand points to 12 at o’clock and the short hour hand points to the hour.

Four A4 pages: a parent guide with a worked example, six clocks to read, six clocks to draw and a complete answer page. All 12 hours are covered.

Try 5–10 minutes together. Your child should recognise numbers 1–12; they can say the answers if writing is difficult. You need a pencil and, optionally, a real clock with two hands. Print pages 2–3 in black and white at actual size.

At 12 o’clock both hands point to 12. A clock face alone does not tell us whether it is morning or evening. Next, practise spotting o’clock times on a real clock before introducing half-hours.
