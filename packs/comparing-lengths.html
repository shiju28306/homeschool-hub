Comparing Lengths with Equal Units

Ages about 5–7

Help your child compare lengths using longer, shorter and the same length, then count equal-sized units.

Four A4 pages: parent guidance, a comparison activity, an equal-unit counting activity and answers. No ruler or cutting is needed.

Try 5–10 minutes together. Your child should be able to count to 8. Read the instructions aloud and accept spoken answers if writing is difficult. Print pages 2–3 in black and white, preferably at actual size.

Count the boxes, not the dividing lines. Use equal-sized units with no gaps or overlaps. These units are not centimetres.

For counting practice, try Numbers 1–10. For another maths activity, explore shapes.
