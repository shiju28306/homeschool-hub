Euro Coins 1c–20c

Junior and Senior Infants · Ages about 4–6

What this teaches: The child learns the coins 1c to 50c, counts totals up to 10c (Junior Infants) or 20c (Senior Infants), and sees that small coins can make a bigger one.

What comes next: Playing shop with the price tags and paying with real coins, then totals up to 50c.

What you need: Real 1c, 2c, 5c, 10c, 20c and 50c coins, a pencil and scissors. Keep coins away from children under 3, as they can be swallowed.
