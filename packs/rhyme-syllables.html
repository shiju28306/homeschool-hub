Rhyme and Syllables: Listening Games

Ages about 3–5

What this teaches: Rhyming words sound the same at the end, like cat and hat. Syllables are the beats in a word: sun has one beat and ap-ple has two. Your child learns to hear both just by listening. There is nothing to read.

What comes next: Make up silly rhymes together and clap the beats in family names, then move on to hearing the first sound in a word with the beginning sounds pack.

What you need: Your voice: say each picture’s name aloud (the names are on the answer page) and clap once for each beat while your child copies you. Also a pencil, crayons and child-safe scissors.
